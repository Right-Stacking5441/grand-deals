How we make money

Who runs this site

grand.deals is run by Hurrell Capital Limited. It is free to use. We pay for it through affiliate links: if you click a shop link and buy something, the shop may pay us a commission. You pay the same price either way.

How links are labelled

Every block of shop links has one line directly above it, starting with an Ad label: Ad · Shop links earn us a commission. It doesn't change prices or order. That covers the price table, the console and bundle list, and each group of product picks. The same disclosure is repeated in the footer of every page. The buttons themselves say Buy, View or Check price, with no separate label, because the line above the block already applies to all of them. Links to PlayStation Direct for the Black DualSense are not affiliate links and earn us nothing.

UK readers

Under the CAP Code, which the ASA enforces, affiliate links are marketing communications and must be obviously identifiable as such. We use the label Ad, placed before the links it covers, and keep the explanation next to every block of links rather than only on this page.

US readers

Under the FTC's Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255), a material connection must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously, close to the links. The disclosure line sits directly above every block of store links on the page. On US pages with Amazon links we also say: As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

How we choose and check

  • We list major retailers in each country, whether or not they pay us. We never list grey-market key resellers.
  • The price table is sorted by price and stock, never by commission.
  • We only show a price that a person has seen on the retailer's own page, with the time we checked it. We re-check by hand twice a day; a row not checked for 16 hours shows Check price instead of a number. If we haven't confirmed a price, the shop is listed under +N more shops, price not confirmed with a Check price link.
  • Amazon prices only appear once they come from Amazon's official Product Advertising API.
  • Product picks are chosen for fit, such as Sony's M.2 rules and Xbox card compatibility. Commission rates don't affect which products we pick.

Programmes

We apply for or use these programmes: Amazon Associates (UK and US), Awin (Currys, Scan), Partnerize (GAME), and the retailer programmes for Argos, Best Buy, Walmart, Target, GameStop, Secretlab and Razer.
